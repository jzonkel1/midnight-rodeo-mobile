## ONLINE PRESENCE AUDIT

### 1. The website that isn't there — mrcrp.com
They own a domain. They use it for email (`mrcrp@mrcrp.com`). Google's Business Profile lists **mrcrp.com** as their website. Typing it in — or clicking it from Google — **301-redirects the visitor to Facebook**. Someone set that up on purpose as a placeholder.

Consequences:
- Google is crawling a domain with zero content to rank. Every keyword their competitors own is unavailable to them.
- Anyone without a Facebook account (or logged out on mobile) hits a login wall instead of a business.
- No page exists for the mechanical bull, the Pour Room, the dance floor, the coffee bar, hours, cover, dress code, or the VIP card.

### 2. Google's AI Overview does not know they exist
Search **"country bar / dance hall / nightclub Corpus Christi."** Google's AI Overview answers:

> "**The Ranch Dance Hall** is the primary active country dance hall and nightclub in Corpus Christi, located at 4653 Everhart Road."

Midnight Rodeo — a 42,200 sq ft country dancehall, the only Midnight Rodeo in Texas — is **not mentioned in the answer at all**. It appears third in the local map results only.
The AI has nothing to cite about them, because there is nothing crawlable to cite. Every fact about this business lives inside Facebook comment threads and one TV news article.

### 3. Reviews — 8 total, and not one of them is from a customer
Google: **4.3 ★ from 8 reviews.** Facebook: **"Not yet rated (0 reviews)."** Every one of the 8 Google reviews was left *before or around opening*:

At the volume this building does on a Saturday, 8 reviews is a rounding error. There is no mechanism in place to ask for one.

### 4. Instagram is abandoned — 96 followers, 2 posts
Facebook has **4.6K followers** and their Reels are doing **110K and 31K views.** The content works. Instagram — the platform their 18–34 country crowd actually lives on — has 96 followers and two posts. The Ranch has 7,400.

### 5. No TikTok account at all
There are TikTok "discover" pages *about* Midnight Rodeo Corpus Christi and videos from customers, but no official account. Other people are building the audience for them.

### 6. Google Business Profile is close to empty
- Category is **"Dance hall"** only — no Bar, Night club, Live music venue, or Cigar bar secondary categories. (Whiskey Rio outranks them partly because it is categorized as *Night club*.)
- **No business description.** No products. No services. No menu. No Google Posts. No Q&A.
- **Sunday shows CLOSED** while their own flyer advertises Sunday Young Country Night, 8 PM–close. Anyone searching Sunday evening is told they're shut.
- Zero drink prices on Google, even though every flyer they publish leads with price.

### 7. The $100,000 problem
100 VIP cards at $1,000 each. There is no page to explain it, no way to buy one, no way to even ask about one except messaging a Facebook page. A person who hears "there's a thousand-dollar VIP card" and googles it finds nothing.

### 8. The Pour Room is invisible
A high-end whiskey bar and cigar lounge is a *different search* than a dancehall — "whiskey bar Corpus Christi," "cigar lounge Corpus Christi." It has no page, no photos indexed, and no category. It's a premium revenue room that no one can find on purpose.

### 9. Renting the audience
4.6K Facebook followers, 110K reel views, and **no email list, no text list, no way to reach anyone off-platform.** When Facebook throttles reach, the room goes quiet. Nothing they've built is theirs.

### 10. No events = no event carousel
A 42,200 sq ft venue with a concert stage and a weekly lineup, and no event calendar anywhere Google can read. That means no Event schema, no eligibility for Google's event listings and "things to do this weekend in Corpus Christi" surfaces — where a venue like this should be a permanent fixture.
