Since the nineties

The only one left in Texas.

In its heyday there were about ten Midnight Rodeos across Texas. Corpus Christi had one at Parkdale Plaza on Staples, and a whole generation learned to two‑step on that floor before the lights went out.

That floor is back. Forty‑two thousand square feet inside the old New Life Church building at Airline and McArdle, rebuilt for a crowd that still knows every word. Same name. Same boots. Bigger room.

“A place where the music never stops, the floor is always moving, and every night feels like the best night out. A blast from the past made for today’s crowd.”

Inside the building

Forty-two thousand square feet of reasons to stay.

Texas-Size Dance Floor

A real wooden floor built for two-stepping, not a patch of tile between tables. There is room for everybody who wants to dance and everybody who wants to watch.

The Mechanical Bull

Eight seconds and a room full of people watching. Somebody in your group is getting on it, and everybody knows who.

Live Country & Concerts

A stage sized for touring acts, not a corner with an amp. When a name comes through Corpus Christi, this is the room that holds them.

Bars You Can Actually Reach

Enough bar length and enough staff that you are not spending your night with your arm in the air. Specials every night we are open.

Coffee & Ice Cream

A real espresso bar and a scoop counter inside the building, because not every reason to come through the door involves a drink ticket.

VIP & Reserved Tables

Your own table, your own space, and a door you do not have to wait at. Ask about tables for birthdays, bachelorettes and big groups.

Inside Midnight Rodeo

The Pour Room.

A high-end whiskey bar and cigar lounge tucked inside the building. Quieter, darker, and a long way from the dance floor when you want the night to slow down for a minute.

Allocated pours, a proper cigar selection, and somewhere to actually hear the person across the table. Pour Room access comes with every VIP card.
